## Really, what is a wave?
- we are familiar with waves in day to day life, but what are they really? how do you formulate them mathematically?
- A wave is an object that has an amplitude and a frequency: a maximum value, and a rate which it's value changes, and it must be periodic (the values repeat at the same rate)

### Types of Waves
- There are transverse waves, and longitudinal waves
- Transverse waves are waves whose amplitude is perpendicular to the direction of travel
	- For example: light is a transverse wave of both $\mathbf{E}$ and $\mathbf{B}$
	- both $\mathbf{E}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ are always perpendicular to the direction of the travel of light
		- this is given by the Poynting Vector: $\mathbf{S}=\frac{1}{\mu_0} \mathbf{E} \times \mathbf{B}$
