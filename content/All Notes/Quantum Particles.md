[[physics]]

- Quanta of electromagnetic radiation in QFT is the photon
	- photons don't interact with each other - why?
		- they are neutral particles
- There are Fermions and Bosons
	- Fermions are the particles, Bosons govern their interaction
	- So something like Gluon which governs quark-quark interaction is a boson, whereas quarks are fermions
	- Other fermions are electron and photon
- What's going on inside a proton?
	- two up quarks and one down quark
	- the interaction between these is governed by the gluon
	- the gluon is an energy, energy can become mass, so the gluon contributes to 75% of the mass of the proton
	- since a quanta of EMR is photons, the interaction between an electron and a nucleus is governed by protons
	- similarly, a quanta of the strong nuclear force is the gluon
