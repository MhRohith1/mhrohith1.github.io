[[quantum physics]]
[[Abstract vector spaces]]

In quantum physics, there are three main unit directions in which the spins of the quantum systems can be in:
- $\ket{u}$ and it's orthogonal vector $\ket{d}$ (up and down)
- $\ket{r}$ and it's orthogonal vector $\ket{l}$ (right and left)
- $\ket{I}$ and it's orthogonal vector $\ket{O}$ (In and out)

Orthogonal:
- $a \perp b \leftrightarrow \braket{a|b}=0$ 

Note that these vectors can be expressed in terms of each other
- Suppose a qubit was prepared to be up. the probability of measuring right with this qubit is 1/2 (that is, 1/2 chance it is right and 1/2 chance it is left)
- Mathematically: $\ket{r} = \alpha_1 \ket{u} + \alpha_2 \ket{d}$, where $\alpha_1 \cdot \alpha_1^{*} = \frac{1}{2}$ and $\alpha_2 \cdot \alpha_2^{*} = \frac{1}{2}$.
- Same holds for all of the other basis vectors
