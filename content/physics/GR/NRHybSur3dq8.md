### Other surrogate models
- There is one surrogate model that they stress in the paper that is 7 dimensional
	- meaning it takes $q$ and two spin vectors
	- this allows them to be accurate on a parameter space $q \leq 2$
	- this model also requires more wave forms
### About Sur3dq8
- Since surrogate models rely on NR that is only generated for 20 orbits before merger, the model in this paper uses hybridized waveforms
	- the last 20 orbits were generated by NR
	- the previous ones generated by Post-Newtonian and EOB formalisms
- Doesn't take into account precession
	- allows for a broader range of $q$
	- less NR waveforms required
- spans the entire LIGO band (what does this mean?)
- Parameter space: $q \in [1,8], |\chi_1|, |\chi_2| \leq 0.8$, total mass of system $\geq 2.25 M_\odot$

### How does the hybridization work?
- First, they develop a surrogate model for Post-Newtonian (PN) waveforms
	- this allows them to deduce the most important parameters in the parameter space (Greedy Algorithm)
- Next, they generate NR simulations over the set of important parameters
- Then, they create PN waveforms over these parameters and stitch the NR-PN waveforms together
	- the PN waveforms become increasingly inaccurate in the phase of the strain, so SEOB[^1] is used to correct the phase
