[[General and Numerical Relativity]]

### Electric and Magnetic Field
- we learn Electric field because it is the most straightforward: it's just field lines that point either to or away from the source of the field
	- Literally think of arrows in space
- This is the electric field of a static charge
- Magnetic field is generated by moving (either with constant or changing velocity) electric charges, and it's direction is based the right hand rule
	- Biot-Savart: 
		- $$\mathbf{B} = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi} \frac{q \mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{r}}{r^3}$$
- IMPORTANT:
	- This is NOT the same as Electromagnetic **radiation**

### Electromagnetic Radiation
- Created by accelerated charges
- This creates a **Radiative** electric field, one that is perpendicular to the vector from the point charge to the observation location
- This electric field propagates outward at the speed of light
- This electric field also has an associated magnetic field
	- This combination carries energy, which is THE photon
- this is not present in the case of a charge moving at constant velocity because there is no propagation
	- the electric and magnetic field will simply reduce in magnitude as the charge moves away from the observation location
