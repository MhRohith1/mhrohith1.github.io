### The standard inner product

### Generalizing the standard inner product
- The Riemannian Metric is a generalization of the inner product
- It is a "space dependent inner product"
	- Essentially, to different points in space, a different inner product is assigned

### Riemannian Metric on Riemannian Manfolds
- Jacobi formulated his Lagrangian on a curved potential well as just $L=K_J$ for some $K_J$ which is a Riemannian Metric, as opposed to the classic definition of $L=K-V$.
	- the inner product needed to define $|\dot{r}|^2$ is space dependent and accounts for the "curvature" of the potential well - this is the Riemannian Metric
	- Both definitions lead to the correct motion of the particle in the potential well
	- The reason Jacobi formulated it this way is because we can bring intuition from Geometry to talk about paths (namely, that an object will follow the shortest path between the configurations $(q_0, t_0)$ and $(q_1, t_1)$) (How?)

### Extending to the Pseudo-Riemannian Metric
- A Riemannian manifold is a structure endowed with a Riemannian metric at each and every point in it
- This can be extended to Pseudo-Riemannian manifolds, which form the backbone of general relativity, as well.
