We take a rectangular prism of a metal, and we ignore interactions between electron-electrons and electrons and nuclei. We model the metal as an infinite square well and we build a "k-space". If we have a state that is excited to values $(n_{x}, n_{y}, n_{z})$ in the $x, y, z$ direction respectively. This builds a k-space:
$$
	k_{i}= \frac{\pi n_{i}}{L_{i}}
$$
where $i$ denotes the coordinates $x, y$ or $z$.
