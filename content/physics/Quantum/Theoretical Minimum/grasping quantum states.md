### So what is different between the classical and quantum world?
- A state in the quantum world is not the same as a state in the classical world
	- State in the classical world: a definite value (heads or tails)
	- State in the quantum world: ????
#### THE EXPERIMENT
- suppose you have a measuring apparatus and a qubit
	- the measuring apparatus determines the spin of the qubit: up or down
- Start by measuring the qubit with the apparatus in the up position
	- the reading you see is that the qubit is "up"
	- this prepares the qubit to be in the up position
		- repeating will only yield "up"
- Now, flip the apparatus so that it is oriented down
	- Now, if you measure on the same qubit, it will give a reading of "down"
	- repeated measurements will yield "down"
- now suppose you oriented the qubit so that it is facing the right
	- Now, if you prepare a qubit, it will read EITHER "up" or "down"
	- if you continue to read the same qubit, it will be the same as the initial measurement
		- this allows us to confirm our measurements
	- however, if you measure with a different qubit, MAY give a different reading!!
	- the average of the "up" and "down" will tend to 0 the more qubits you use
