[[Spin]]
### Introduction
- As was alluded to in the section on [[Spin]], it is not simple to combine two particles with different angular momenta together
- Ultimately, we will rely on the Clebsh-Gordan table to combine the spins in a way that preserves quantum mechanical rules
### A simple example
- Suppose we trapped two spin 1/2 particles in a box
- We can either measure the total angular momenta of the box, or the individual angular momenta
- We develop two formalisms: one for capturing the total angular momenta of the system, and one for capturing each one individually
- ###### Notation:
	- Since these are just two spin 1/2 particles, we will denote the two particles and their possible states as follows:
$$
\ket{++}, \ket{--}, \ket{+-}, \ket{-+}
$$
- The first symbol denotes the spin of the first particle, while second symbol denotes the spin of the second particle
