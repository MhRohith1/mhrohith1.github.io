# Rohith Muthukumar's Digital Garden

This repository powers my personal website and digital garden, built with Quartz v4. It collects my notes, presentations, and project material across physics, mathematics, scientific computing, and machine learning.

I am Rohith Muthukumar, a Physics and Mathematics student at The University of Texas at Austin. My current interests include gravitational physics, condensed matter physics, plasma physics, scientific computing, and machine learning. I work with the Center for Gravitational Physics on extrapolating the MAYA catalog of gravitational-wave waveforms using polynomial extrapolation in `1/r`.

The site includes my public notes on general and numerical relativity, classical mechanics, quantum mechanics, machine learning, and mathematics. It also links to selected presentations from directed reading projects on the Pauli matrices, Cauchy-characteristic evolution, and differential topology.
